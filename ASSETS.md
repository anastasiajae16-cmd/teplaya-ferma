# Игровая графика

Файл проекта: dist/assets/farm-atlas-v2.png (1536×1024, PNG с альфа-каналом).
Создан встроенным image_gen, не через API/CLI. Собственная графика проекта; предоставленный пользователем скриншот служил ориентиром композиции, его игровые изображения не извлекались.

Финальный промпт:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D farm game sprite atlas. Create EXACTLY TWELVE individual hand-painted 2.5D isometric farming objects laid out in a mathematically uniform 4 COLUMN by 3 ROW GRID on a 1536x1024 canvas. Each cell is 384px wide and 341px high. IMPORTANT: every object must occupy ONLY the central 70% of its OWN cell. Keep at least 40px of fully transparent EMPTY padding on EVERY edge of EVERY cell, especially between rows. No object may cross a cell boundary. No ground tiles, no grass, no background color, no cast shadow extending beyond its object, no UI, no words, no grid lines. Consistent beautiful detailed cozy social farming game art, vibrant green foliage, warm terracotta wood, visible 3D texture, fixed elevated isometric camera. Row 1: apple tree bearing red fruit; pear tree bearing yellow fruit; fir tree; lilac bush. Row 2: red wooden barn; wooden windmill with FOUR COMPLETE sails fully inside its cell; stone bakery with terracotta roof and smoking chimney; wooden chicken coop with ramp. Row 3: friendly black and white dairy cow; white chicken; low wooden fence section; stack of golden hay bales. Keep consistent styling and fully transparent backgrounds, these are production sprite assets. All four objects in each row share the exact same row center, and all three objects in each column share the same column center. No decorative extra objects. Large transparent gaps between sprites essential.

Трава, грядки, растения, цветники, вода, дорожки и интерфейс рисуются кодом Canvas/CSS. Иконки HUD — системные эмодзи.

Production environment texture for a polished classic social farming browser game. A 1536x1024 landscape image, full-frame elevated isometric / axonometric camera looking down at a SUNNY GRASSY FARM CLEARING. No horizon, no sky, no perspective vanishing point. Richly detailed painterly 2.5D game art matching warm wooden farm sprites, attractive vibrant yellow-green grass with individual blades, subtle clover, small wildflower tufts, natural soft dappled shadows. The terrain MUST follow this layout because interactive objects will be added in code: A narrow warm sandy cobblestone walking lane starts at 49% image width and 22% image height, descends gently through (49%,50%), bends to (55%,66%) and exits bottom at (55%,100%). A branch leads from (49%,50%) right through (66%,47%) to (81%,56%). At lower right there is an irregular small turquoise pond centered at (88%,78%), about 22% image width and 23% image height, surrounded by natural grassy banks, rounded stones, reeds and subtle water ripples. The remaining land MUST be OPEN grass for separately rendered farm objects: no buildings, no animals, no trees, no crop beds, no tools, no signs, no labels, no UI. Upper and far side margins have sparse beautiful tiny wildflowers and clover, not large bushes. Keep the middle-left clear for interactive fields, middle-right clear for buildings. Ground texture should be detailed and organic, not flat vector, not geometric ellipses, not a screenshot, no shadows shaped like nonexistent buildings. Crisp professional game background, warm cheerful daylight, no text anywhere.
